# Restaurant OS — staged execution

Initial format confirmed by the user: **dine-in + takeaway + delivery**. Scope includes every workspace and capability in the uploaded feature brief and all 54 catalogue requirements in the research report. This is a staged product build; a visible screen does not establish that every specialist workflow is complete.

## Stage 1 — foundation and identity

Implemented: React/TypeScript app, Node/Express API, persistent SQLite/WAL database, tenant/legal entity/outlet schema, session authentication, password hashing, role/outlet checks, CSRF and origin checks, user administration, password change and administrator reset with forced change, deactivation/reactivation and role/outlet reassignment, session listing/revocation, encrypted TOTP enrolment with short-lived challenge login/replay protection and one-use recovery codes, outlet amount/percentage approval policies and expiring named-user delegations, audit, ingredient/table/outlet setup, branded responsive interface. Fig/Pear colours and self-hosted Manrope/Fraunces follow the supplied guide. The actual logo masters were not uploaded; the UI uses a typographic product name instead of recreating the logo.

Implemented organisation extensions: legal entity/brand/stock-location registers, outlet entity/brand assignment before operational history, custom stations used by menu routing/KDS.

Remaining gates: reviewed Arabic translation and RTL, official logo masters, jurisdiction-specific operator setup, independent security assessment, production encryption-key provisioning and MFA enforcement/recovery policy. Physical lot placement/moves between configured locations belong to Stage 3. Self-service email recovery requires an authorised notification provider. Specialist formats do not gain regulatory approval by selecting a setting.

## Stage 2 — connected restaurant service

Implemented: POS, item search/categories, notes, price and recipe snapshots, ingredient-aware modifiers, required/max modifier validation at API, branch availability, separate order/payment states, kitchen station routing and timers, delay reasons, consume-on-preparation, full-station readiness, handover/close, pre/post-preparation cancellation, remake, original-tender partial refunds, split tenders, immutable bill amount/item/seat allocations with payment against individual part balances, seat/course metadata and independent course hold/fire, whole-order hold/resume, ownership transfer, versioned/idempotent amendments with immutable tickets and balanced financial differences, item/quantity packing and remake recheck, table transfer history, reservation conflict/capacity checks in UAE time, capacity-aware combined seating with protected release, waitlist contact/seating/departure lifecycle, booking deposit receipts/application/original-source refunds and outstanding finance register, guest pickup/delivery and private tracking, live refresh events, durable signed offline drafts with device registration/revocation, safe replay and conflict review, independent variant families and fixed component/modifier combos with operator administration, aggregate stock/allergens, station routing and online/offline dependency-version checks.

Remaining gates: physical printer hardware validation and device fallback runbooks. Per-unit sharing of one item across seats, configurable deposit/no-show/forfeiture terms, closed-till financial corrections and controlled prepared-item corrections after refunds/splits are implemented and tested: a settled prepared item can only be reduced by a manager/owner/accountant who records evidence, the charged difference is refunded through the controlled refund path, and the consumed portion of the unsent food is reclassified as an Inventory loss — never silently restored to stock — with an idempotent correction ledger (`line_corrections`) that preserves the immutable ticket. An offline application shell now caches the interface for offline startup; kitchen/floor tickets are queued, confirmed or retained on interruption with a controlled reprint. Unprepared line removals and post-preparation additions are implemented; prepared food is never silently restored. Offline drafts do not represent accepted orders or authorised card payments.

## Stage 3 — supply, ingredients and production

Implemented: supplier approval, purchase approval, inspected partial receipts with lot/expiry/evidence, stock reservation and FEFO, negative-stock blocking, quarantine/release permissions, stock counts/variance approval, waste/disposal, multi-input batch issue with immutable source lots, production-in-progress accounting and balanced output rounding, actual-versus-planned yield/quality hold/output release, source-expiry guards and evidenced expiry correction, inherited prepared-component allergens, source expiry retained across internal outlet transfers, movement ledger and recursive lot trace through recorded batches/transfers to reserved/consumed orders, scoped recall-chain quarantine and ancestor release guards, evidence files. Supplier purchase packs convert pack quantities and costs to base units and cost-per-base-unit at purchase creation, with a mismatch against the chosen supplier or ingredient blocked. A receipt records an inspection outcome and a rejected quantity; rejected stock is logged as a non-usable movement and never creates a lot, and accepted-plus-rejected quantities close the purchase balance. Supplier bills match received net value within an owner-configurable fixed-plus-percentage tolerance; a within-tolerance variance posts an explicit Purchase price variance line under authorised review, while an outside-tolerance difference is blocked. Versioned, nested recipe/BOM sub-recipes define a prepared output ingredient with immutable revision history, suspend guards, recursive explosion into consolidated base ingredients (cost, availability/shortfall and inherited allergens) and recipe-driven production planning that creates a planned batch from a recipe's immediate inputs.

Remaining gates: reviewed shelf-life/label procedure and regulator-certified recall completion, both requiring clinical/regulator sign-off. Multi-line purchase orders, receipt quality rules, individual bulk-service consumer identity, recall review and notification-provider selection are implemented (notice transmission still needs a live provider). Supplier offer comparison, explicit ingredient-substitution approval, demand/capacity planning linkage, multi-level base-ingredient batch consumption, physical stock placement/moves and intercompany transfer treatment are implemented.

Remaining gates: payment gateway/terminal authorisation and signed callbacks; bank and real marketplace-statement ingestion; validated payroll/WPS export; period-close sign-off by a pilot accountant. A transactional fiscal-period close (`closeFiscalPeriod`) now finalises a date range no later than the current business date: every closed date is blocked by the posting guard, all open orders and till sessions across the range must be resolved first, and the close is audited and reported (`fiscalPeriods`). The default 5% UAE illustration is **not** a tax assessment. Manual bank evidence is not an online bank integration.

## Critical controls

One event creates one order; one tender key records one payment. Reservations block overlapping bookings. Cashiers cannot release held stock; refunds require configured role limits or a valid scoped delegation. Expired/held/reserved stock cannot be silently consumed. A refund never restores food. Original prices/recipes remain immutable. Remakes issue more stock. Transfers reduce source stock before destination acceptance. Issued financial evidence cannot return to draft. Every journal balances in integer fils. Exports and files obey scope. Offline status remains pending/failed until the server accepts it. Loyalty balances derive from movements. Safety deviations create accountable tasks. AI recommendations are distinct from verified events.

These controls are implemented where listed above and exercised in the existing tests. Broader acceptance is tracked in REQUIREMENTS.json and the complete supplied FEATURE-BRIEF.md. Each pending feature remains part of scope; it is not omitted because its screen exists.
