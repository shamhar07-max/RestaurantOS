### Purchasing packs, rejected receipts and matching tolerance — 2026-10-11

Added a supplier **purchase packs** register and pack-based purchase ordering. A purchase created from a pack derives base-unit quantity and cost-per-base-unit transactionally and rejects a pack whose supplier or ingredient does not match the order. Partial receipts now capture an inspection outcome (pass/conditional/fail) with separate accepted and rejected quantities: rejected stock is recorded as a non-usable `receipt_rejected` movement with no lot, accepted stock creates a lot and posts a balanced receipt journal, and accepted-plus-rejected quantities close the order balance. Supplier bills match received net value within an owner-configured fixed-plus-percentage tolerance exposed through `GET /api/settings` and an owner-only `POST /api/settings/purchase-match`; a within-tolerance variance posts an explicit Purchase price variance line under authorised review, and an outside-tolerance difference is blocked.

New backend checks cover pack conversion and mismatched supplier/ingredient rejection, manual base-unit fallback, rejected stock remaining unavailable while accepted stock is usable, receipt over-balance and pass-with-rejection guards, tolerance default/variance posting/journal balance, and owner-only policy administration over HTTP.
